# Marketing Campaign Analysis -- Insights Summary

## Executive Summary

This project analyzed marketing campaign performance using a 9-step SQL
pipeline. The goal was to identify how marketing budgets should be
allocated for maximum ROI. The data revealed that individual channels,
segments, and locations show minimal differences. However, meaningful
insights emerged when combining channel and customer segment data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Findings

### 1. Funnel Performance

CTR ≈ 10% and Conversion ≈ 8% across all channels. This means engagement
efficiency is uniform.

### 2. ROI vs Conversion

ROI is not driven by conversion rate alone. All channels show similar
ROI (\~5).

### 3. Underperforming Campaigns

No extreme low performers. Relative benchmarking (below average ROI) is
required.

### 4. Channel Performance

All channels have nearly identical ROI. No single channel can be labeled
as a winner.

### 5. Customer Segments

Segments perform similarly. Segmentation alone does not drive ROI.

### 6. Geographic Performance

Locations show minimal ROI differences.

### 7. Pareto Analysis

Email, Google Ads, and Website slightly outperform others. Differences
remain small.

### 8. Micro-Segmentation

Combining Channel + Customer Segment reveals the best results: -
Facebook + Tech Enthusiasts - Website + Foodies - Google Ads +
Fashionistas

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Final Recommendation

Marketing budgets should be allocated based on channel--segment
combinations rather than channels alone. Continuous testing and cost
optimization should be the focus.
